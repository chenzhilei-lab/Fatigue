一、摘要全域重写（彻底砍掉工程导向表述）
原问题表述（全部删除替换）
删掉原句：量化齿轮三大标准方法诱导不确定性、为标准统一 / 齿轮设计提供依据、给出预测散差工程范围、设计师可优先管控某类选择等工程化描述。
新摘要标准模板
This work develops a full-factorial censored-likelihood AFT variance decomposition framework to quantify methodological uncertainty from multiple competing fatigue calculation rules for mechanical components with right-censored run-out data. Unlike traditional ANOVA/Sobol methods that discard unobserved failure lives, the proposed likelihood ratio partitioning retains all censored samples and achieves consistent factor ranking even at high censoring ratios (up to 58%). A single numerical spur gear model (42CrMo4, \(m_n=3, z_1=20\)) is adopted solely as a demonstration testbed to illustrate the framework’s workflow, covering five categorical methodological switches: fatigue standard size/surface formulas (ISO/AGMA/FKM), surface roughness, mean stress corrections, S-N curve datasets, and damage criteria. All numerical variance fractions and life divergence ranges are pure simulation outputs without physical test calibration, which cannot be applied to gear design or standard revision. Sensitivity analyses verify the stability of factor ranking under varying load coefficients, surface conversion ratios, residual stress states, and model distributions (log-normal/log-logistic/Weibull). This paper’s sole contribution is the statistical UQ pipeline for censored full-factorial fatigue datasets; the gear case only serves to showcase implementation, and no generalizable engineering conclusions on gear standard discrepancy are drawn.

This study proposes a censored-likelihood AFT full-factorial variance decomposition method for fatigue datasets with abundant run-out observations. The framework avoids the systematic underestimation of dominant factors existing in conventional ANOVA/Sobol analysis when censoring ratio exceeds 20%.
A modular simulation pipeline compatible with multiple international fatigue calculation specifications (ISO, AGMA, FKM) is established, and a virtual spur gear model is used exclusively to verify the implementability of the proposed statistical workflow. All gear-related numerical results are uncalibrated simulation data with no physical experimental support and carry no engineering guiding significance.
Comprehensive sensitivity tests on model distribution, stress baseline, surface conversion coefficient, residual stress input and load parameters confirm the robustness of the factor ranking output by the likelihood partitioning method.
The presented UQ framework is transferable to other machine component fatigue problems (shafts, bearings, welded joints) with categorical calculation choices and censored life data; future work will extend the model to nonlinear cumulative damage formulations and more component testbeds.
