Limitation of factorial design asymmetry: The present design heavily oversamples methodological decision space (5 multi-level factors) while only incorporating two S-N datasets from a single 42CrMo4 material heat. The conclusion that "method-induced uncertainty dominates prediction divergence" is only valid for this narrow material sampling setup and cannot be generalized to designs with multiple material heats, batches or alloy types. Quantitatively separating material vs method uncertainty requires expanding the material factor with ≥3 independent experimentally fitted S-N curves for follow-up study.
